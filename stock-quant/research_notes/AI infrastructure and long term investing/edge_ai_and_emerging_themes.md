## 1. Edge AI adoption and sizing: smartphones, AI PCs, NPUs, automotive and industrial/IoT edge inference, and who captures the value

### Takeaway
Edge AI did not drive a device upgrade cycle in 2026. Instead, an AI-driven memory shortage shrank the device market. IDC (Aug 26, 2026) expects a record 16.7% fall in 2026 smartphone shipments, with ASPs up 27.6% to $581, and worldwide PC shipments turned negative from Q2 2026. The AI-capable share keeps rising almost by default (44% of US PC shipments in Q1 2026, per Omdia). The money from "more AI per device", however, went to memory makers rather than edge SoC vendors. Micron's Aug-2026 quarter revenue rose 379% YoY at an 81% operating margin. Qualcomm's Sep-2026 quarter revenue fell 8.4% YoY, and its operating margin halved from 26% to 13%.

### Inferences
- **In 2026 the edge-AI story was about content and price, not units.** If IDC is right that memory prices keep rising into 2028, the phone and PC "AI upgrade cycle" will stay price-led rather than volume-led through at least 2027. The most exposed are SoC vendors and OEMs with low-end Android exposure: MediaTek's and Qualcomm's lower tiers, and Xiaomi. Apple's premium mix and scale look like an advantage: revenue +11.7%, and the stock closed at a 52-week high on Nov 13, 2026.
- **Memory makers effectively collected the toll for on-device AI in 2026.** The stocks already price a peak, though. MU trades at 3.4x Yahoo forward P/E and sits 50% below its Jun 25 high despite record results. For 2027, the question is whether value shifts back to SoC and IP vendors as memory supply normalises.
- **"AI PC" is turning into a default spec rather than a differentiator.** The AI-capable share rises while total units fall, so share data alone is a weak investment signal for 2026–27.
- **The strongest *verified* revenue acceleration in edge silicon is in industrial/IoT/auto MCUs, analog, low-power FPGAs and IoT connectivity.** It coincides with a cyclical inventory recovery: Microchip's operating margin went from 8% to 29% in four quarters and TI's from 37% to 48%. That growth is therefore not proven to come from AI.
- **China's edge-AI SoC and IP ecosystem is growing fast** (Rockchip, VeriSilicon), but the equities round-tripped: VeriSilicon is down 79.6% from its Jun 30, 2026 high [Yahoo 688521.SS](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/688521.SS).

## 2. Edge AI and physical AI companies: fundamentals, valuations, returns, latest-quarter highlights and risks

### Takeaway
In edge-related silicon, real reported revenue momentum sits in two places. The first is analog, MCU and auto/industrial chips in a cyclical upturn: TI +27.7%, ADI +39.6%, Microchip +42.2%, NXP +19.3% and Renesas +30.0% YoY in their latest loaded quarters. The second is a few small caps: Lattice +118%, Silicon Labs +21.8% and Ambiq +110%. Qualcomm, the purest handset and edge-SoC franchise, is shrinking (-8.4%) but cheap at 15.1x forward P/E. Arm (67.6x forward P/E, 29x EV/sales) and the stalled names (Mobileye, Ambarella, CEVA) are priced on narrative rather than growth. In physical AI, incumbents have real revenue and profits: Intuitive Surgical, Fanuc, Keyence, Cognex and Hesai. Most humanoid, robotaxi and robot pure plays fell 60–90% from their highs; Rainbow Robotics, at -47%, is the main exception. That equity theme peaked between November 2025 and January 2026.
