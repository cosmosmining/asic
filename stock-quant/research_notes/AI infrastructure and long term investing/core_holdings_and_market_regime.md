## October-November 2026 drawdown

### Takeaway
- **Size:** From the 2026-10-06 record, the S&P 500 fell **11.6% to a 2026-11-20 closing low (6,910.39)**. On 2026-11-25 SPY stood about **8% below** its 52-week high (716.70 vs about 779). QQQ was about **17% below** its high, because the sell-off was concentrated in AI and megacap growth stocks.
- **Volatility:** The decline came with very large daily swings: SPY −4.4% on Nov 5, −3.6% on Nov 20 and +4.7% on Nov 23.
- **Causes** (each sourced below):
  1. re-accelerating inflation: hot October CPI and PCE, plus the legacy of the spring energy shock
  2. a hiking Fed and a 10-year yield above 5% for much of November
  3. doubts about the return on about $730B of 2026 hyperscaler capex, with negative reactions to Q3 results (Mag-7 missed estimates in aggregate)
  4. Nvidia's CEO and governance shock
  5. policy risk: midterm gains for Democrats (AI regulation) and new tariff plans
- **Not an earnings recession:** Consensus CY2026/2027 EPS growth kept rising. This was a de-rating: the forward P/E went from 22.2 to about 17.
- **Rotation:** Leadership moved to energy, health care, staples and dividend stocks.
- **Not retrieved:** VIX levels and named sell-side 2027 outlooks (see Gaps).

### Inferences
- **What kind of correction this was:** It was driven by discount rates and valuation, and concentrated in AI and long-duration growth stocks. It was not an earnings recession: consensus earnings rose while the forward multiple compressed about 5 points (22.2 → about 17) as Fed hikes and a 10-year yield above 5% raised the discount rate.
- **Depth tracked AI exposure:** QQQ was about −17% versus SPY about −8% on Nov 25 (coordinator data), while energy, health care, staples and SCHD were +21% to +36% over 12 months. Equal-weight, value/dividend and defensive sleeves provided real diversification. The deeper QQQ drawdown, against −14.5% for the Nasdaq Composite, fits the Nasdaq-100's heavier megacap/AI weighting.
- **High realized volatility:** There were three daily moves of 3.6–4.7% in 13 sessions (Nov 5–23), plus a 4.6% intraday Nasdaq swing on Nov 25.
- **Trend filters have turned negative:** SPY, QQQ and VTI are below their 10-month moving averages (coordinator data), so the widely used 10-month trend filter is currently "off" for US equities. Its historical hit rate (protective exits vs whipsaws) was not quantified this session.
- **Near-term swing factors (dated):**
  - Dec 8–9 FOMC
  - Dec 10 CPI
  - clarity on Nvidia's leadership and governance
  - 2027 hyperscaler capex guidance (early 2027)
  - tariff implementation
- **Lesson for a multi-decade core:** The episode is a live example of concentration risk (top 10 about 39%). It supports holding diversifiers (equal weight, value/dividend, international, and T-bills yielding about the 3.75–4.00% policy rate) rather than relying only on cap-weighted US growth.

## 1. Market regime now: index levels and returns, valuation, concentration, Fed/10-year/CPI, 2026 events, EPS consensus

### Takeaway
As of late November 2026, the US is in a **re-inflation plus Fed-tightening plus AI de-rating** regime:
