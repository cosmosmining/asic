Method, data and caveats
  • Prices are daily adjusted closes from Yahoo Finance, so returns include dividends. Non-US listings are in local currency and are left out of backtests.
  • Backtests decide at each month-end close and trade the next day, charge 10–15 basis points per unit traded, and pay a T-bill rate on cash.
  • The AI-infrastructure list was chosen in 2026. Backtesting it from 2016 rewards hindsight: companies that failed or were never noticed are missing. Treat those returns as an upper bound.
  • Goal odds assume returns are independent from year to year. Real markets have streaks and crashes, so the bootstrap table replays actual history as a check.
  • Valuation flags: stretched means forward P/E above 60 or EV/sales above 20; premium means forward P/E above 35; peak-cycle risk means a forward P/E under 12 on sales growth above 75%, a pattern that often marks the top of a boom.
  • Regenerate everything with the stock-quant toolkit: fetch, report, then dashboard_export.
