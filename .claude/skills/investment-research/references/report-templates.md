# Report templates

Keep notes skimmable. Each claim carries a date and a source link. Numbers from the
bundled scripts cite "quant snapshot, <date>".

## Single-name note

```markdown
# <Company> (<TICKER>): <one-line verdict>
_As of <YYYY-MM-DD>. Research, not personal advice._

**Verdict:** <Core candidate | Satellite candidate | Watchlist | Speculative only>, sized <x–y%>.
**Why, in three lines:** <business quality>; <valuation vs growth>; <trend and risk>.

## Snapshot
| Price | From 52w high | 12m | 1y volatility | Worst 1y drop | Forward P/E | EV/sales | Revenue growth (latest qtr) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Latest results and guidance
## Valuation
## Risks and bear case
## Catalysts and review triggers
| Date | Event | Upgrade if | Downgrade if |
|---|---|---|---|
## Entry plan and sizing
## Sources
```

## Theme report

```markdown
# <Theme>: <one-line conclusion>
_As of <YYYY-MM-DD>. Research, not personal advice._

## Bottom line
- Is the demand real? <evidence with numbers>
- Who captures the profits? <links of the chain with pricing power>
- What is priced in? <basket performance, valuation, bubble score x/20>
- How to own it: <ETF or basket, size, entry plan>

## Value chain map
## Demand and financing
## Universe
| Ticker | Company | Link in chain | Stage | Theme share of revenue | Latest growth | Valuation flag | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
## Real revenue vs narrative
## Market pricing (theme screen)
## Bubble checklist (score x/20)
## Historical analogues
## Portfolio fit: sizing, entry, kill criteria
## What to watch next
## Sources
```

## Chat summary (after saving the file)

Five to eight lines: the verdict, the three numbers that matter, the main risk, the
entry plan, and where the full note is saved.
